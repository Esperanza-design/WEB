Taller: Crea tu bowl

Workshop: Create your bowl

Descubre el arte de la cerámica creando tu propio bowl desde cero en un taller práctico y creativo. Aprenderás a trabajar el barro paso a paso, moldeando, dando forma y personalizando tu pieza hasta convertirla en algo único. Una experiencia relajante y diferente donde conectarás con tus manos, la calma y la satisfacción de crear algo propio.

Discover the art of ceramics by creating your own bowl from scratch in a hands-on, creative workshop. You'll learn to work with clay step by step, molding, shaping, and personalizing your piece until it becomes something truly unique. A relaxing and different experience where you'll connect with your hands, find calm, and experience the satisfaction of creating something of your own
