Taller: Crea tu taza

Workshop: Create your mug

Descubre la cerámica elaborando tu propia taza desde cero en un taller práctico y creativo. Aprenderás a modelar la arcilla paso a paso, dar forma a la pieza, incorporar el asa y decorarla hasta conseguir un objeto único hecho por ti. Una actividad tranquila y diferente que te permitirá desconectar, trabajar con tus manos y disfrutar del proceso de crear algo completamente personal.

Discover the art of ceramics by creating your own mug from scratch in a hands-on, creative workshop. You'll learn to model clay step by step, shape the piece, add the handle, and decorate it until you have a unique object made by you. This relaxing and unique activity will allow you to disconnect, work with your hands, and enjoy the process of creating something truly personal.
